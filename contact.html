About Me

My name is Jacob Lancaster, and I am the owner of Stickman Handyman. With 6 years of hands-on experience, I have built my business around reliable service, consistent workmanship, and clear communication.

I recently completed my engineering degree at CU Boulder, and I bring that same problem-solving mindset to every project. I focus on practical, long-lasting solutions and take pride in doing the job right.

Looking ahead, I plan to grow Stickman Handyman into a full-service home improvement company — expanding the team, taking on larger renovation projects, and continuing to serve the northern Colorado community with the same quality and reliability that has defined my work from day one.
